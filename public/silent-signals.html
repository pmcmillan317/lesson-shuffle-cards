The move

Groups work on one task on a shared sheet of paper without speaking, using only writing, sketches and arrows.

  1. Set the rules1 minute“For the next five minutes, no talking. You can write, draw, add arrows, ask questions on the paper and answer each other on the paper.”

  3. Model if neededFirst timeWrite a question on one sheet and draw an arrow to an answer.

Why it works

In spoken discussion, the fastest talkers set the direction. On silent paper, everyone writes at once, quieter students get equal space, and ideas can be answered with arrows and questions. This works like the “Chalk Talk” protocol many schools use.

Watch out for

  • Whispering: pause the timer; restart when it’s silent.
  • Students writing only in their corner: require at least one arrow to someone else’s idea.
  • Running out of ideas: add a second question in the middle at the halfway point.

Level up

  • Stretch the silence a minute longer each round.
  • Each student must answer a question someone else wrote.

Make it work for everyone

  • Multilingual learners: writing and drawing give time to think before speaking.
  • IEP and 504 supports: allow sticky notes stuck on the paper instead of writing directly.
  • Early finishers: circle the idea they’d defend and explain why.

Tech option

A shared digital whiteboard works the same way.
