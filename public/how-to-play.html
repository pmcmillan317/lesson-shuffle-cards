One card. One strategy. One better lesson.

Lesson Shuffle is a deck of ready-to-use strategy cards that spark engagement, collaboration and thinking without disrupting your lesson plan. Pick a card to add a student-centered twist to any activity.

Why use it

  • High-impact, low-prep routines
  • Works with any lesson, any subject
  • Boosts engagement and student thinking
  • Grounded in research on what works

How to play

  1. Pick a cardDraw at random, or choose one while you plan.
  2. Scan the QR codeEach card links to a short guide with the rationale, steps and lesson ideas.
  3. Try it in classStart small. Most cards take under 10 minutes and little or no prep.
  4. ReflectWhat worked? What would you change? Then try the card’s Level up challenge.

Reading a card

  • Category (top left): which of the four suits the card belongs to.
  • Difficulty (top right): 1 to 3 apples. More apples means more planning or practice.
  • Challenge (dark bar): the card’s goal in a few words.
  • Rationale and steps (card body): why it works and what to do, ending with a Level up.
  • Resources (QR code): the full guide for that card.

The four suits (13 cards each)

  • Instructional Delivery: how you present and pace new learning.
  • Collaboration & Discussion: how students talk, share and build on each other’s ideas.
  • Assessment & Retrieval: how you check understanding and help it stick.
  • Classroom Systems & Equity: routines and structures that work for every student.

Wildcards

  • Perfect Pair: draw two cards and use both, in the same lesson or on back-to-back days.
  • Take Five and Lunch Off-Limits: teacher well-being cards. Play them for yourself.
  • Submit a Card Idea: have a strategy that works? Scan it and share it with Lesson Shuffle.

House rules

  • Double Down. Replay your last card and change one small thing: the timing, who works together, or the materials. Notice what shifts.
  • Instruction first, tool second. Use technology when it adds real value. If paper works just as well, use paper.

Triple Plays: three cards that work well together

  • Dialogue and Accountability (amplify student voice): Circle Up Check, Think Pair Rotate Share, Every Hand Up.
  • Clarity and Momentum (sharpen learning): Focus Frame, Ten-Po Shift, Flow Finder.
  • Active Processing and Feedback (build retention): Brain Busters, Boards Up!, Glow Grow Go.
