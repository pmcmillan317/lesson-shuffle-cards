The move

Students book a different classmate for each hour on a clock, so you can call “Meet your 3 o’clock!” and pairs form in seconds.

Step by step

Step timing

  5. 5Move30 seconds
  6. 6Missing partner15 seconds

Setup (once, 10 minutes)

  1. Hand out the sheet1 minute
  2. Sign up8 minutes“Find someone with the same hour open. Write each other’s names. Each name only once.”
  3. Fill gaps1 minuteHelp anyone with blanks. Collect a photo or copy for absent days.

Every use (1 minute)

  1. Call the hour5 seconds“Grab your clock. Meet your 3 o’clock!”
  2. Move30 secondsCount down from 30.
  3. Missing partner15 seconds“Hand up if your partner’s out.”Pair those students together.
  4. Give the task

See how it works

See it in action

Use 4 hours (12, 3, 6, 9). “Meet your 6 o’clock and read your story to them.”

“Meet your 9 o’clock and compare your answers to number 4.”

“Meet your 11 o’clock and debate the prompt for three minutes.”

Watch out for

  • Lost sheets: keep a class copy, or have students tape it inside a notebook.
  • Avoided partners: if a pair doesn’t work, quietly swap one hour.
  • Slow movement: count down every time.

Level up

  • Race the clock to see how fast pairs form.
  • Run a second sign-up mid-year with a new clock.

Why it works

Random pairing every time is slow and can feel awkward; fixed partners get stale. Clock Partners gives students twelve pre-agreed partners, so you get variety without the chaos, and students work with classmates outside their usual circle.

Make it work for everyone

  • Multilingual learners: during sign-up, make sure each student has at least one partner who shares their home language.
  • IEP and 504 supports: pre-fill a few hours with supportive partners.

Tech option

Students can keep a photo of their clock on a device.
