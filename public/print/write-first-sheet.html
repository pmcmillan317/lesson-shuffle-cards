Write First SheetCollaboration & Discussion · For Write First

How to use it

  1. 1Print one per two students and cut in half.
  2. 2Post the question. Start a two-minute timer: pens moving the whole time.
  3. 3Pairs share from their writing, word for word, before the class talks.
  4. 4After the discussion, students revise one sentence.

Cut into two half sheets.

Students · 1 page, US Letter · black-and-white friendly
