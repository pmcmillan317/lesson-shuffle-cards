Feedback Flow Comment SheetCollaboration & Discussion · For Feedback Flow

How to use it

  1. 1Print one per student.
  2. 2Each student leaves their draft and sheet at their seat.
  3. 3Post the round’s focus question. Readers move to a new draft each round; authors stay put.
  4. 4Run three rounds, 3 to 4 minutes each. One reader, one comment, then move.
  5. 5Save the last few minutes for authors to pick a comment and revise.

Students · 1 page, US Letter · black-and-white friendly

Feedback Flow Comment Sheet

This sheet stays next to the draft. Readers rotate; the author stays put.

Directions
  1. 1Reader: read the draft and answer only this round’s focus question.
  2. 2Write one thing you noticed and one thing to try. Initial it, then move on.
  3. 3Author: at the end, pick the comment you’ll use.
