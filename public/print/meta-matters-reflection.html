How to use it

  1. 1Print one per two students and cut in half.
  2. 2Hand it out with the graded quiz, before students look at the score too long.
  3. 3Students answer all three, then pick one strategy to try.
  4. 4Before the next quiz, ask who tried their strategy and how it went.

Cut into two half sheets.
