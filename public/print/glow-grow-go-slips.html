Glow, Grow, Go SlipsAssessment & Retrieval · For Glow Grow Go

How to use it

  1. 1Print one sheet per three students and cut into slips.
  2. 2Post today’s goal so students can copy it.
  3. 3At the end of a task, students fill one slip about their own work, or a partner’s.
  4. 4Collect them. Skim the Grow lines for what to reteach. Go lines show what students plan to do.

Cut into three slips.

Students · 1 page, US Letter · black-and-white friendly
