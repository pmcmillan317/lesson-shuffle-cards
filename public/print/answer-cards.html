How to use it

  1. 1Print on cardstock, one sheet per student.
  2. 2Cut along the dashed lines and keep each set in an envelope or clipped together.
  3. 3Ask a multiple-choice question. Students hold their pick against their chest.
  4. 4On “Show me,” everyone flips at once. Scan the room before you say a word.

Shapes: A circle, B triangle, C square, D star.

Students · 1 page, US Letter · black-and-white friendly
