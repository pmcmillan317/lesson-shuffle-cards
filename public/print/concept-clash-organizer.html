Concept Clash OrganizerInstructional Delivery · For Concept Clash

How to use it

  1. 1Print one per two students and cut in half.
  2. 2Post the pair: “How is a ___ like a ___?” Odd pairs work best.
  3. 3Students write alone for two minutes, then talk with a partner.
  4. 4Take three answers. Ask where each comparison breaks.

Cut into two half sheets.

Students · 1 page, US Letter · black-and-white friendly
