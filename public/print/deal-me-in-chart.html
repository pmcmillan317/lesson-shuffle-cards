Deal Me In Chart

Hand every student a playing card. Then group by suit, split by color or call a number.

By suit

Four groups: hearts, diamonds, clubs and spades. Best for small-group work.

Say: “Find the other students with your suit.”

By color

Two halves: reds and blacks. Best for two-sided tasks, debates and quick pairs inside each half.

Say: “Reds on this side, blacks on that side.”

By number

Each number has one student from every suit, so every group has a voice. Use it to pick who answers.

Say: “All the sevens, share your answer.”

Build your deck

One card per student. Use every number in all four suits: ace through 6 for 24 students, ace through 7 for 28, ace through 8 for 32. With an odd count, add a joker as a wild card that joins any group. Collect and reshuffle every day.
