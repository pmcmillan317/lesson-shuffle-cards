How to use it

  1. 1Print one sheet. Cut along the dashed lines, or enlarge each slip on a copier to poster size.
  2. 2Write a prompt on each slip, or tape a piece of student work on it.
  3. 3Tape one slip at the top of a sheet of chart paper at each stop, or post it as is. Use four to six slips.
  4. 4Groups write responses in their own marker color. Rotate on your signal.

Teacher · 1 page, US Letter · black-and-white friendly
