How to use it

  1. 1Print one sheet per two words, per student.
  2. 2Say the word, give a kid-friendly meaning, and show one example and one non-example.
  3. 3Students write their own sentence about their lives and sketch the word.
  4. 4Revisit each word at least twice this week.
