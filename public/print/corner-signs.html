Corner SignsCollaboration & Discussion · For Move & Speak

How to use it

  1. 1Print four pages, one per corner, on cardstock if you can.
  2. 2Before class, write one position on each sign (or stick a note on it).
  3. 3Tape one sign in each corner, high enough to see over heads.
  4. 4Read the question, then students walk to their corner and build their best argument together.

Prints four pages.
