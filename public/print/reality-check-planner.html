How to use it

  1. 1Print one per group.
  2. 2Pose one local problem students can see, like cafeteria waste or hallway traffic.
  3. 3Give about 10 minutes for the four boxes.
  4. 4Each group pitches in one minute. Send the best fix to someone who can act on it.
