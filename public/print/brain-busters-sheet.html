Brain Busters SheetAssessment & Retrieval · For Brain Busters

How to use it

  1. 1Print one per two students and cut in half.
  2. 2After a lesson segment, name the topic and start a 60-second timer.
  3. 3Students check their notes and add what they missed in a different color.
  4. 4Partners compare and find what both forgot. Reteach that.

Cut into two half sheets.

Students · 1 page, US Letter · black-and-white friendly
