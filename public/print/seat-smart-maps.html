How to use it

  1. 1Print the page and post it at the front, or cut it into three posters.
  2. 2On the first day, call a name: “Clusters, go!” Students push their desks to match the diagram.
  3. 3Remind them: lift, don’t drag.
  4. 4Time the first move, write the time on the board, and try to beat it next time.
