How to use it

  1. 1Print one sheet per three students and cut into slips.
  2. 2Post three questions: one from last week, one from last month, one from last unit.
  3. 3Students answer from memory, then check and fix in a different color.
  4. 4Twice a week is plenty.

Cut into three slips.
