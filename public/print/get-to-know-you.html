How to use it

  1. 1Print page 1 for every student. Page 2 is your private tracker.
  2. 2Give it in the first weeks. Skip any question that doesn’t fit your students.
  3. 3Each week, pick one student and use one detail to start a short chat in passing.
  4. 4Keep answers private. To use a Google Form instead, copy the questions into a new form.
