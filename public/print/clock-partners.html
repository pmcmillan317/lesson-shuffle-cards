How to use it

  1. 1Print one per student on regular paper.
  2. 2Give about 10 minutes, once, for students to fill all 12 hours.
  3. 3To pair up, call a time: “Find your 3 o’clock partner.”
  4. 4Students keep it in a folder. Redo it each semester.
  5. 5Younger students: use only 12, 3, 6 and 9.

Clock Partners

Twelve ready-made partners, set up once. To rotate, your teacher calls a time.

Directions
  1. 1Stand up with this sheet and a pencil.
  2. 2Find a classmate with the same hour open. Write each other’s names on that hour.
  3. 3Each name goes on your clock only once.
  4. 4Fill as many hours as you can. Ask your teacher to help with any blanks.
