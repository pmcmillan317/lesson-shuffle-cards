Map It Out TemplateAssessment & Retrieval · For Map It Out

How to use it

  1. 1Print one per student, or let students copy the layout onto a blank page.
  2. 2Students close their notes and write the terms in the boxes at the top.
  3. 3Still from memory, they draw arrows between related terms and write a few words on each arrow, like “causes” or “is part of.”
  4. 4Then they open their notes and fix the map in a second color: add missing arrows and correct labels.

Students · 1 page, US Letter · black-and-white friendly
