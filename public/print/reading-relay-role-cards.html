Reading Relay Role Cards

One set per group of four. After each section, pass your card to the left.

Summarizer

Your job: Say the most important idea of this section in one or two sentences.

Say: “This part was mostly about…” / “The key idea is…”

Questioner

Your job: Ask the group one question the text answers, and one it makes you wonder.

Say: “Who can find where it says…?” / “I wonder why…”

Clarifier

Your job: Find a word or idea that could be confusing and help the group figure it out.

Say: “A tricky word was ___. I think it means ___ because…”

Predictor

Your job: Use clues from the text to guess what comes next.

Say: “I predict ___ because the text says…”
