Text Talks BookmarksCollaboration & Discussion · For Text Talks

How to use it

  1. 1Print one sheet per four students. Cut into four bookmarks.
  2. 2Draw a line or stick a note at each stopping point in the text before class.
  3. 3Give one bookmark per student. Partners read to the line, stop, and each say something.

Cut into four.

Students · 1 page, US Letter · black-and-white friendly

Text Talks Bookmarks

Each partner keeps one in the text while reading.

Say something

At the line, stop. Each partner picks one before you read on.

  • Predict “I predict… because…”
  • Question “Why did…?” “How does…?”
  • Connect “This connects to…”

Don’t use the same move two stops in a row.
