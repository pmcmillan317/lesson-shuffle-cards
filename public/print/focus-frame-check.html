How to use it

  1. 1Print one sheet per three students and cut into slips.
  2. 2Post today’s goal and two or three “I can” statements.
  3. 3Check them at the start, middle and end of class.
  4. 4Collect the slips. Lots of 1s on the same line? Start there tomorrow.
