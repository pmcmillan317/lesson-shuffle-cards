The move

At the end of a task, students write one Glow (what went well), one Grow (what to improve) and one Go (their next step).

Step by step

Step timing

  1. 1Set it up20 seconds
  2. 2Glow1 minute
  3. 3Grow1 minute
  4. 4Go1 minute
  5. 5Collect30 seconds
  1. Set it up20 secondsWrite GLOW, GROW, GO on the board, next to today’s goal or success criteria.“Look back at what you just made, and at today’s goal.”
  2. Glow1 minute“One thing that went well. Point to it, and name the goal it meets.”
  3. Grow1 minute“One thing that could be better.”
  4. Go1 minute“One action you’ll take next time. Start with a verb.”
  5. Collect30 secondsSticky notes on the door, or paper in a tray, so you can read them.

Why it works

Useful feedback answers three questions: How am I doing? What needs work? What do I do next? Glow, Grow and Go cover the same ground: what’s working, what needs work, what’s next. So students practice judging their own work and leave with a plan instead of a vague feeling.

Watch out for

  • Vague glows (“it was good”): require pointing to a line or a spot.
  • Go steps that aren’t actions: start with a verb.
  • Never reading them: skim the Go notes and respond to two the next day.

Level up

  • Trade with a partner and add a Glow to theirs.
  • Check last week’s Go: did you do it?

Make it work for everyone

  • Multilingual learners: starters: “I did well at…” / “I can improve…” / “Next I will…”
  • IEP and 504 supports: allow a sketch, or a quick verbal version to you.
  • Early finishers: write a second Go.

Tech upgrades

Free tools that change the task, not just the medium.

  • ModifyGoogle Forms exit ticketGlow, Grow and Go fields feed a Sheet. Filter the Grows to plan tomorrow’s reteach groups.
  • ModifyRunning reflection docEach student keeps one Google Doc of Glow/Grow/Go entries all semester; version history shows their growth.

AI prompts

Copy, fill in the [brackets] and paste into Gemini, ChatGPT or Claude. Leave out student names and personal details.

Make the materials

I teach [grade] [subject]. We are working on [topic]. Write sentence starters for Glow, Grow and Go that fit [type of task] in [grade], and 3 strong and 3 weak examples of each so I can model the difference. Before you finish: Strong examples must point to specific evidence in the work. Write at a level my students can read. If anything above is unclear, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
