The move

For one lesson, you stay away from the front of the room and teach by moving among students.

Step by step

Step timing

  1. 1Launch from the side2 minutes
  2. 3Coach, don’t rescue30–60 seconds per stop
  3. 5Close from the floor2 minutes
  1. Launch from the side2 minutesGive directions from the side or back of the room.“Today the front of the room is lava. I’ll be coming to you.”
  2. Set a routeDuring work timeMove in a loop so you reach every table every 5–10 minutes.
  3. Coach, don’t rescue30–60 seconds per stopLook at the work, ask one question, move on.“Show me where you got stuck. What could you try next?”
  4. TrackOngoingJot names and quick notes on a clipboard so you know who to revisit.
  5. Close from the floor2 minutesShare one common mistake and one strong example you saw.

Why it works

When you circulate, you see student work as it happens, catch mistakes in the moment and give feedback right where it’s needed. Your presence near students also keeps more of them on task without a word.

Watch out for

  • Long stops at one table: keep each visit under a minute.
  • Missing the back corners: start your loop there.
  • Needing the board: write on a student’s whiteboard or a sticky note instead.

Level up

  • Let a student run the board while you roam.
  • Leave a sticky-note comment at every desk before the lesson ends.

Make it work for everyone

  • Multilingual learners: visit early so they hear directions again one-on-one.
  • IEP and 504 supports: plan your route to reach students with accommodations first.
  • Early finishers: carry extension cards to hand out as you go.

Tech upgrades

Free tools that change the task, not just the medium.

  • ModifyGoogle Forms on your phoneA 3-question form (student, what I saw, next step) logs each stop. In the response Sheet, conditional formatting highlights students you haven’t reached this week.
  • AugmentClassroomscreen (free)Put the work timer and task on the board so students never need you at the front to know what to do.

AI prompts

Copy, fill in the [brackets] and paste into Gemini, ChatGPT or Claude. Leave out student names and personal details.

Make the materials

I teach [grade] [subject]. We are working on [topic]. I will teach [lesson] from the floor, not the front. List the 3 most likely places students get stuck on [skill], and for each give one coaching question I can ask at a desk that helps without giving the answer. Before you finish: Make every question answerable in under a minute. Write at a level my students can read. If anything above is unclear, ask me up to 3 questions before you start.
