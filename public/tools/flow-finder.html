    How to use it

    1. Pick one cue for every switch, like "Finish. Stand. Shift." Teach it once and practice.
    2. Preview the next task in 10 seconds. Then press Chime and start and say the call.
    3. The timekeeper presses Everyone's ready: stop when the last student is ready, then writes the time on the board next to the class record.
    4. If it took over a minute, calmly practice that switch once more. Keep going until switches are under a minute.
