Keys: Space think, then pick · P pick now · F phone a friend

Set up your classes

First names are enough. Paste from a spreadsheet column.

Your class lists are saved in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere, and there is no sign-in. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

How to use it

  1. Add your names once under Set up your classes. Each class keeps its own list.
  2. Ask the question and say "No hands." Press Think, then pick so everyone answers in their head first.
  3. Read the name. If they're stuck, press Phone a friend: they pick a classmate to help, then say the answer in their own words.

Shuffle. Pick. Grow.
