Your bank is saved in this browser only. Nothing you type leaves this device, and there is no sign-in. Clearing your browser data deletes it, and it does not follow you to another device. Press Export backup now and then to keep a copy, and Import to load it back.

How to use it

  1. Post the three questions. Pick ones most students can answer with some effort. If almost no one can answer one, reteach it before asking again.
  2. Say "No notes. Answer all three. Guess if you're not sure." Give about 2 minutes, silent, on paper.
  3. Press Reveal answers. Students fix their own in another color and do not erase.
  4. Optional: tally each by hands up. Press Class forgot this on any the class missed, so you can find it in your bank and reteach.
