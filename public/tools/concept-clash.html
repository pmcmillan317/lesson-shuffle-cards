Concept Clash spinner

Spin for an everyday object, then ask how today's concept is like it. Think alone first, then tell a partner.

Ready?

30

    Add today's concept under Set up, then spin.

    Keys: Space or S spin · N next step (think, partner, close) · T partner timer

    Set up your spinner

    Type one idea, or a few vocabulary words (one per line) and one is picked each spin. Commas stay part of the text.

    Keep them concrete and familiar, with "a" or "an" (a ladder). Each one comes up once before any repeats.

    Copies your concept and object list into a link you can paste to open this setup on another device.

    Your concept and list are saved in this browser only. Nothing you type leaves this device, and there is no sign-in. A setup link holds your text in the link itself, so share it only where you would share the list.

    How to use it

    1. Model one pairing yourself first, aloud: one way they are alike and where it stops working.
    2. Type today's concept under Set up, then press Spin. Read the question aloud and tell students to think on their own first. They may jot a few words.
    3. When the think timer ends, students tell a partner one way the two are alike and where the comparison stops working. Press Start partner timer if you want it timed.
    4. Take three answers. After each, ask the class: "Does that comparison hold up? Where does it stop working?"
    5. Press Close and have students finish the sentence frame to keep the idea in mind.

    Shuffle. Pick. Grow.
